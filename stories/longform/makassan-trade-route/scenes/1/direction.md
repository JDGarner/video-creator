# Scene 1 · Before Australia was colonised

**Timecode:** 0:00--0:40  
**Purpose:** Establish the central surprise and hook the viewer.

## Visuals and animation

-   Open on a dark, atmospheric map of the world.
-   Zoom towards Southeast Asia and northern Australia.
-   Fade out modern country borders and highlight the Arafura Sea.
-   Introduce an animated traditional sailing vessel travelling south.
-   Brief title card: **Centuries before British colonisation**.
-   Cut between the map and atmospheric footage or stills of Arnhem
    Land's coastline.

## Narration

What if I told you that Australia was already part of an international trading network long before the British arrived?

For centuries, fleets of Indonesian sailing vessels travelled thousands of kilometres across the seas to northern Australia.

They came for a valuable marine delicacy, destined for the markets of China.

But this wasn't simply a story about fishing.

It was a relationship between two very different worlds --- one that would leave lasting traces in Australia's landscape, languages and Indigenous cultures.

## Production notes

-   Make the opening map immediately legible: Southeast Asia, northern
    Australia and the Arafura Sea.
-   Avoid suggesting the entire Australian continent was connected to
    this trade in the same way. The focus is northern Australia,
    especially Arnhem Land.
