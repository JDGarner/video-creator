# Scene 5 · Processing trepang

**Timecode:** 2:45--3:35  
**Purpose:** Make the industry tangible and explain why processing camps were established.

## Visuals and animation

Use a clear four-stage illustrated process diagram:

1.  **Harvest:** Collect sea cucumbers from coastal waters.
2.  **Boil:** Gut and boil them in large iron pots.
3.  **Process:** Cool, clean and prepare the catch.
4.  **Preserve and export:** Dry or smoke the product for transport.

Show a coastal camp with: - A cooking area and cauldrons. -
Fireplaces. - Processing and drying areas. - Praus anchored offshore.

## Narration

Once the fleets arrived, the real work began.

Sea cucumbers were collected from shallow coastal waters and brought back to processing camps.

There, they were gutted and boiled in large iron cauldrons.

They were then cooled, cleaned and dried or smoked.

This process transformed a perishable marine animal into a durable export commodity that could survive the long voyage back to Indonesia.

The finished trepang was transported to Makassar, where it entered established trading networks supplying Chinese markets.

What had begun as a seasonal fishing expedition was, in reality, a sophisticated international industry.
