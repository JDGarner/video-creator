# Scene 7 · More than a trading relationship

**Timecode:** 4:20--5:00  
**Purpose:** Show the material, social and human dimensions of contact.

## Narration

Over time, the relationship developed well beyond the exchange of sea cucumbers.

The Makassans brought goods that were valuable to Aboriginal communities, including metal tools, fishing equipment, textiles and pottery.

Metal was particularly significant, providing materials that could be adapted for everyday use.

But the exchange wasn't just material.

Some Aboriginal people travelled back to Makassar aboard the visiting vessels.

There were opportunities to experience foreign places, meet different communities and establish relationships that extended across the Arafura Sea.

The connection between northern Australia and Indonesia had become something much more complex than a commercial transaction.
