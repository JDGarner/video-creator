# Scene 6 · Aboriginal communities already living there

**Timecode:** 3:35--4:20  
**Purpose:** Establish the Indigenous context and avoid an outsider-centred narrative.

    than invented dramatic scenes.

## Narration

But the Makassans were not arriving in an empty wilderness.

Northern Australia was home to Aboriginal communities with deep connections to their Country, established systems of trade and exchange, and extensive knowledge of the surrounding seas.

Among them were the Yolŋu people of Arnhem Land.

The arrival of the Makassan fleets created opportunities for contact, negotiation and exchange.

These relationships were not simply between outsiders and passive local populations.

Aboriginal communities actively participated in the encounters, trading resources, sharing knowledge and developing relationships with the visiting crews.
