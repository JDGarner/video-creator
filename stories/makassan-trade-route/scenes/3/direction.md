# Scene 3 · Who were the Makassans?

**Timecode:** 1:20--2:00  
**Purpose:** Introduce the people, their home port and their vessels.

## Visuals and animation

-   Locate Makassar on a map of Sulawesi in present-day Indonesia.
-   Zoom into an illustrated harbour.
-   Show traditional wooden sailing vessels being prepared.
-   Animate crews loading supplies, equipment and cooking pots.
-   Label the vessels *praus* or *prahu*.

## Narration

The people behind these voyages were known collectively as Makassans.

They came primarily from the region around Makassar, on the island of Sulawesi, in what is now Indonesia.

The crews included people from different communities in the region, including Makasar and Bugis sailors.

They were experienced maritime traders, familiar with the seasonal winds and currents of the Indonesian archipelago.

Their vessels were wooden sailing ships called praus.

Each year, crews prepared their vessels, gathered supplies and set sail southwards towards the Australian coast.

They were not exploring an unknown land at random. They were returning to a place that had become part of an established commercial route.

## Production notes

-   "Makassan" is a broad historical label for participants in the
    voyages, not necessarily one single ethnic identity.
-   Use historically appropriate vessel references. Do not automatically
    depict modern tourist phinisi boats as exact replicas of every
    historical prau.
