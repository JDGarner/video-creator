## Narration

For generations, these voyages connected Indonesia and northern Australia.

But eventually, the industry came to an end.

The last recorded Makassan prau visited Arnhem Land in 1907.

The end of the trade came amid changing colonial policies and restrictions on the movement of foreign fishing fleets.

A relationship that had continued across generations was brought to a close.

The ships stopped coming. But the cultural connections they had created did not simply disappear.
