## Narration

The story is also written into the physical landscape.

Along the Arnhem Land coast, archaeologists have identified the remains of former Makassan processing camps.

Some contain stone arrangements associated with fireplaces used to boil trepang.

Archaeologists have also recovered fragments of pottery, glass and metal objects.

In the late 1960s, historian Campbell Macknight surveyed and excavated a number of these sites.

His work helped reconstruct how the camps operated and established an important archaeological foundation for understanding the trade.

His 1976 book, *The Voyage to Marege'*, brought together archaeological evidence, historical documents and interviews with people connected to the voyages.
